ChatPack Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026 · Applies to ChatPack 0.6.1 open edition and editions with Premium activation.

What ChatPack does

ChatPack is a local-first browser extension for creating conversation and notebook archives. It runs only after you invoke it on the active tab and asks the browser for access to that tab for the requested capture.

Information handled

To create an archive, ChatPack reads page content mounted in the active tab and processes it in your browser. Archive content, message text, attachments represented in the page, and verification evidence are stored locally in the browser. Depending on the selected page, this content may include names or other identifying details and private communications. Exports are generated on your device.

Information we receive

The 0.6.1 open edition does not send conversation content, archive files, verification records, browsing history, cookies, account credentials, or authentication tokens to ChatPack. It does not include analytics or a developer-server connection.

Storage and deletion

Your archives remain in your browser and files you export remain wherever you save them. You can remove local archive data from ChatPack or clear the extension's browser storage. Deleting an archive in ChatPack does not delete a file you previously downloaded.

Third-party services

ChatPack is independent of the AI websites you visit. Those services apply their own privacy practices to their accounts and pages. ChatPack does not read cookies or intercept account or network tokens.

Payments and Premium licenses

ChatPack Premium is offered as a one-time purchase for ¥12.90 through Afdian and payment services selected by that platform. Afdian handles checkout, payment confirmation, and refund requests under its terms. The ChatPack developer does not directly collect or process card numbers, bank credentials, or payment-account passwords.

To redeem and activate a purchase, the ChatPack service receives the private redemption token, a browser-generated retry secret, the activation code, and an extension-generated installation identifier. The service stores hashed redemption and activation values, ticket status and timestamps, and up to two installation identifiers and signed licenses per purchase. A short-lived, keyed hash of the network address is used for rate limiting; API requests purge rate-limit windows older than five minutes. The licensing service does not receive conversation text or archive files.

One Premium code can be bound to at most two browser installations. Refund requests are handled through Afdian. After a refund, the seller can revoke the corresponding redemption record to block future claims or activations; a signed offline license already issued cannot be remotely recalled.

Changes and contact

The open edition remains local-first and does not include Premium activation. The optional commercial edition uses the licensing data practices described above; conversation content still stays in your browser and is not sent to the licensing service. For privacy questions, use the ChatPack support page. Support requests use a public issue tracker; do not post conversation text, private screenshots, account credentials, authentication codes, or other sensitive information.

ChatPack 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日 · 适用于 ChatPack 0.6.1 开放版及包含 Premium 激活功能的版本。

ChatPack 的用途

ChatPack 是一款本地优先的浏览器扩展，用于归档 AI 对话和 Notebook。只有在你主动调用时，它才会请求读取当前活动标签页，以完成你发起的捕获。

处理的信息

创建归档时，ChatPack 会读取当前活动标签页中已加载的页面内容，并在你的浏览器内处理。归档文本、页面中呈现的附件信息和完整性证据保存在浏览器本地；根据所选页面内容，归档可能包含姓名等身份信息或私人通信。导出文件也在你的设备上生成。

我们收到的信息

0.6.1 开放版不会向 ChatPack 服务器发送对话内容、归档文件、校验记录、浏览历史、Cookie、账户凭据或认证令牌；此版本不包含分析统计或开发者服务器连接。

存储与删除

归档保存在你的浏览器中，导出的文件保存在你选择的位置。你可以在 ChatPack 中删除本地归档，也可以清除扩展数据。删除归档不会删除之前下载的文件。

第三方服务

ChatPack 独立于你访问的 AI 网站。相关网站的账户和页面仍受其自身隐私规则约束。ChatPack 不读取 Cookie，也不拦截账户或网络认证令牌。

付费与许可证

ChatPack Premium 为一次性购买，价格为 ¥12.90，通过爱发电及其选择的支付服务完成交易。爱发电依据其平台条款处理结账、付款确认和退款申请。ChatPack 开发者不会直接收集或处理银行卡号、银行凭据或支付账户密码。

领取和激活购买权益时，ChatPack 服务会收到私密兑换令牌、浏览器生成的重试密钥、激活码和扩展生成的安装标识。服务保存兑换与激活信息的哈希值、授权记录状态及时间戳，并为每笔购买最多保存两个安装标识和对应的签名许可证。服务还会短暂使用网络地址的密钥哈希进行频率限制；API 请求会清除超过五分钟的限流记录。授权服务不会收到对话文本或归档文件。

一个 Premium 激活码最多可绑定两个浏览器安装环境。退款申请通过爱发电办理。退款后，卖家可以撤销对应兑换记录，以阻止后续领取或激活；已经签发的离线签名许可证无法远程收回。

政策更新与联系

开放版仍为本地优先版本，不包含 Premium 激活功能。可选的商业版本按照上文说明处理授权数据；对话内容仍保存在浏览器内，不会发送给授权服务。如有隐私问题，请访问 ChatPack 支持页面。支持请求会发布在公开问题跟踪页；请勿提交对话文本、私人截图、账户凭据、认证码或其他敏感信息。
