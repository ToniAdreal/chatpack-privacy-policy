ChatPack Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Applies to ChatPack 0.6.0 open edition.

What ChatPack does

ChatPack is a local-first browser extension for creating conversation and notebook archives. It runs only after you invoke it on the active tab and asks the browser for access to that tab for the requested capture.

Information handled

To create an archive, ChatPack reads page content mounted in the active tab and processes it in your browser. Archive content, message text, attachments represented in the page, and verification evidence are stored locally in the browser. Depending on the selected page, this content may include names or other identifying details and private communications. Exports are generated on your device.

Information we receive

The 0.6.0 open edition does not send conversation content, archive files, verification records, browsing history, cookies, account credentials, or authentication tokens to ChatPack. It does not include analytics or a developer-server connection.

Storage and deletion

Your archives remain in your browser and files you export remain wherever you save them. You can remove local archive data from ChatPack or clear the extension's browser storage. Deleting an archive in ChatPack does not delete a file you previously downloaded.

Third-party services

ChatPack is independent of the AI websites you visit. Those services apply their own privacy practices to their accounts and pages. ChatPack does not read cookies or intercept account or network tokens.

Changes and contact

This policy applies to the free open edition identified above. If a future edition adds account, payment, or license services, its data practices and this policy will be updated before that edition is offered. For privacy questions, use the ChatPack support page. Support requests use a public issue tracker; do not post conversation text, private screenshots, account credentials, authentication codes, or other sensitive information.

ChatPack 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 28 日 · 适用于 ChatPack 0.6.0 开放版。

ChatPack 的用途

ChatPack 是一款本地优先的浏览器扩展，用于归档 AI 对话和 Notebook。只有在你主动调用时，它才会请求读取当前活动标签页，以完成你发起的捕获。

处理的信息

创建归档时，ChatPack 会读取当前活动标签页中已加载的页面内容，并在你的浏览器内处理。归档文本、页面中呈现的附件信息和完整性证据保存在浏览器本地；根据所选页面内容，归档可能包含姓名等身份信息或私人通信。导出文件也在你的设备上生成。

我们收到的信息

0.6.0 开放版不会向 ChatPack 服务器发送对话内容、归档文件、校验记录、浏览历史、Cookie、账户凭据或认证令牌；此版本不包含分析统计或开发者服务器连接。

存储与删除

归档保存在你的浏览器中，导出的文件保存在你选择的位置。你可以在 ChatPack 中删除本地归档，也可以清除扩展数据。删除归档不会删除之前下载的文件。

第三方服务

ChatPack 独立于你访问的 AI 网站。相关网站的账户和页面仍受其自身隐私规则约束。ChatPack 不读取 Cookie，也不拦截账户或网络认证令牌。

政策更新与联系

本政策仅适用于上述免费开放版。未来版本若增加账户、支付或授权服务，我们会在提供该版本前更新相关数据实践说明和本政策。如有隐私问题，请访问 ChatPack 支持页面。支持请求会发布在公开问题跟踪页；请勿提交对话文本、私人截图、账户凭据、认证码或其他敏感信息。
