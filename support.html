ChatPack Support

For a product question or bug report, use the ChatPack public issue tracker.

Protect your privacy

Issues are public. Do not include conversation text, private screenshots, names or contact details, account credentials, authentication codes, or archive files. A useful report can include the ChatPack version, browser version, AI platform, the capture stage, and a short description of the problem.
